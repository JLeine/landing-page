Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany. When you access the site, the hosting provider automatically processes the following information in server log files:

  • IP address of the requesting device
  • Date and time of the request
  • Name and URL of the requested file
  • Referrer (the page from which the request originated)
  • Browser type and version, and operating system

This processing is necessary to deliver the website, ensure its security and stability, and troubleshoot faults. The legal basis is Article 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in the secure and reliable operation of the website). The logs are deleted after a short period, once they are no longer needed for these purposes.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. No third-party content (such as fonts, scripts or embedded media from external providers) is loaded, and no user profiles are created.

External links

This website links to profiles on LinkedIn and XING. These are ordinary hyperlinks: no data is transmitted to those providers unless you click a link. Once you follow a link, the privacy policy of the respective provider applies.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • obtain access to the personal data processed about you (Article 15)
  • request rectification of inaccurate data (Article 16)
  • request erasure of your data (Article 17)
  • request restriction of processing (Article 18)
  • data portability (Article 20)
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests (Article 21)

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The competent authority for this website is the Hamburg Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (HmbBfDI).
